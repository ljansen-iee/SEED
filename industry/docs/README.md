## 🏗️ Model Architecture

### Sector Types

The model handles two types of sectors differently:

#### **Detailed Sectors** (5 sectors)
- **Steel, Ammonia, HVC, Cement, Paper**
- Have explicit production data and technology pathways
- Formula: `Energy = Production × Technology_Route × Intensity × Fuel_Share`
- Volume CAGR: From SSP scenarios and national plans
- Efficiency CAGR: From engineering studies (IEA, literature)

#### **Simple Sectors** (8 sectors)
- **Food, Construction, Machinery, Mining, Non-ferrous, Textile, Transport equip, Wood**
- Lack detailed production data
- Formula: `Energy = BaseEnergy × Activity_Growth × Efficiency_Gain × Fuel_Share`
- Volume CAGR: From UNSD historical data (with IQR capping)
- Efficiency CAGR: Scenario-based assumptions (⚠️ need validation)

## 🎯 Key Methodological Innovations

### 1. **IQR Outlier Capping** (Simple Sectors)
- Removes statistical outliers from historical CAGR calculations
- Handles COVID-19 impacts, data errors, policy shocks
- Provides robust estimates even with noisy data
- Implementation: `industry/core/cagr_calculator.py`

### 2. **Separated Activity & Efficiency** (All Sectors)
- Explicit separation of production growth from intensity improvements
- Allows independent scenario assumptions for each
- Detailed sectors: Both modeled explicitly
- Simple sectors: Mathematically decomposed from total energy trends

### 3. **Technology-Specific Pathways** (Detailed Sectors)
- Different efficiency rates by technology route
- Example: Steel BF+BOF vs. DRI+EAF have different improvement potential
- Enables realistic technology transition modeling

## ⚠️ Known Limitations & TODOs

### High Priority

1. **Simple Sector Efficiency Rates** - Currently placeholder values
   - TODO: Validate against IEA Energy Efficiency Indicators
   - TODO: Add country-specific efficiency potentials
   - See: [EFFICIENCY_CAGR_SUMMARY.md](EFFICIENCY_CAGR_SUMMARY.md), Section "Simple Sectors"

2. **UNSD Data Gaps** - Some countries missing from historical data
   - TODO: Implement regional fallback averages
   - Currently uses 1.5% blanket default
   - See: [VOLUME_CAGR_SUMMARY.md](VOLUME_CAGR_SUMMARY.md), Section "Fallback Behavior"

3. **Production CAGR Sources** - Need better documentation of SSP scenario mapping
   - TODO: Document exact SSP-scenario to production-growth mapping
   - TODO: Add country development stage classification

### Medium Priority

4. **Circular Economy** - Material recycling not yet modeled
   - Would reduce production demand for materials
   - Important for plastics, metals, paper

5. **Policy Responsiveness** - Growth rates static, not policy-responsive
   - TODO: Link to carbon pricing, efficiency standards
   - TODO: Dynamic scenario adjustments

### Low Priority

6. **Sub-sector Disaggregation** - Simple sectors very aggregated
   - Example: "Machinery" covers diverse equipment types
   - Future: Break into sub-categories with different growth patterns
